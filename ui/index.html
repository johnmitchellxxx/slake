How it works
  1. Name your lounge and tell people what you're called.
  2. Send the link by text or email. Click the lounge name to copy it.
  3. They open the link, pick a name, and they're in.

Messages go straight between people's own devices, and nothing is kept on our server. A lounge lasts as long as people stay in it. When everyone leaves, it's gone.

Chat that lives on your friends' computers. No server, no bill.

Already use Slake? Same name and password, and your rooms come with you.

No server, so your account lives only on your devices. Why?

Slake has no server, so your account lives only on your own devices.

As long as one of your computers stays logged in, you can just log in anywhere else with your name and password, and your account (your rooms, messages, name and picture) will be copied over. That one computer needs to be on and running Slake at that moment.

If you log out of everything, your account is gone for good. Nobody can bring it back, not even us.

Phones and browsers can clear their storage without asking, so don't count on them. Install the desktop app and leave it logged in to keep your account safe. Or just be a leaf. Either way.
