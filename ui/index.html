Already use Slake? Same name and password, and your rooms come with you.

No server, so your account lives only on your devices. Why?

Slake has no server, so your account lives only on your own devices. As long as one of your computers stays logged in, you can log in anywhere else with your name and password (that computer just needs to be on and running Slake at the time).

If you log out of everything, your account is gone for good. Nobody can bring it back.

Phones and browsers can clear their storage without asking, so don't count on them. Install the desktop app and leave it logged in to keep your account safe.
