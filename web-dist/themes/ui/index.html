How it works
  1. Type a space name, then Make or Join. Anyone who types the same name lands in the same space.
  2. Send the link by text or email. Click the space name to copy it.
  3. They open the link, pick a name, and they're in.

Messages go straight between people's own devices, and nothing is kept on our server. A space lasts as long as people stay in it. When everyone leaves, it's gone.
